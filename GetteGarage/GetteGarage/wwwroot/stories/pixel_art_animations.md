# Game Animations

A handful of animations I made for my games, all made in aseprite. Pixel animation is a funny medium where if you misplace a single pixel, it looks wrong and you have approximately 16 pixels and a handful of colors to convey weight, personality, and some action.

## Big Larry

Larry's one-handed swing started with a video of a friend swinging a wiffle bat on the lawn, which became my reference. Larry's hats and bats are animated in separate layers so any combination can be swapped depending on what the player has equipped. There are 9 frames in his swing animation and getting the rally cap to look right as it's turning in space was a little funky. You can read about that in the blog: [I made and published a game in six weeks!](/blog/i-made-and-published-a-game-in-6).

## Henry and Trash Bandit

These animations I tried to push myself to get something dynamic in just a few pixels. Henry has the backpack silhouette moving back and forth as he runs and the raccoon I added a front flip and handstand to emulate Mario from DK '94.
