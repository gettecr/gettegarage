## The Premise

You volunteer at an abandoned golf course. Instead of turning it into a farm
or a resort, you restore it into a bird sanctuary. Plant native
vegetation, wait for the habitat to mature, and see who shows up.

It's a cozy ecological restoration game with a bird photography habit,
inspired by Stardew Valley, Pokémon Snap, and Viva Pinata.

## Build It and They Will Come

Every bird species has different needs, so what you plant decides who
visits. A patch of grassland brings in different birds than a stand of
shrubs or a wetland edge.

## Pack Light

You don't carry an endless backpack. You carry a **field kit**, with
separate loadouts for seeds, restoration structures, recording gear,
and hand tools. You prepare before you head out, then work with what you
packed. Switching tools feels a bit like swapping weapons in an action
game, only the weapons are shovels and binoculars.
