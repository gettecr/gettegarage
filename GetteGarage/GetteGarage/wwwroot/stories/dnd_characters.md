# DrawABox

I started doing [DrawABox](www.drawabox.com) and was merrily working my way through drawing 250 boxes when I suddenly got bored. Maybe I don't have the discipline to draw that many boxes. Or maybe I discovered I like to paint. Either way, I decided to paint all of the characters in my DnD campaign and give them to my friends. What I didn't anticipate was that oil takes a long time to dry so this "little project" would take me almost 6 weeks to finish. I did finish it though, so maybe I should get back to drawing boring old boxes.

These are painted on card stock, 2.5"x3.5" with oil. Both sides are painted and all of our characters are barbarians. 

**Art:** The Barbarian Forest Gnome. He thinks he's a rock gnome. 

**Thalmor:** The Barbarian High Elf. A barbarian with wild magic and hates bad smells.

**Chanel:** The Barbarian Desert Tabaxi. She loves a good string toy. With a blade on the end.

**Dorgothy:** The Barbarian Nun with a tendency to overheat and get rather sweaty. And is annoyed about it.

**Argon:** The Barbarian because our DM loves barbarians. He's not of the party, yet is always present. 

**Erdric:** The Barbarian Knight and 3' 6" Harengon. Don't make fun of his fluffy tail.
