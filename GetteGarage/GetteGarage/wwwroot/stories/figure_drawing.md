# Sketchbook

I grew up wanting to draw comics like Mike Hawthorne (Spiderman; Daredevil) or Todd McFarlane (Spawn) and I aspire to create illustrations and paintings like Frank Frazetta, James Gurney, or Michael Whelan. So I scribble in my notebook, make pixel art, and putter here and there with figures and portraits. 

Instead of hiding them in a closet (where most of my sketchbooks end up eventually) I wanted to at least share a few.
