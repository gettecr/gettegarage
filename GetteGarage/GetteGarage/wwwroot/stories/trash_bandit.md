
## Born From Getting Stuck

This one started as a distraction. I'd been stuck on a spawn algorithm
for my main project, For the Birds, for a while, the creative muse
left, and I did what any reasonable developer would do: abandoned it
and started something new.

## A Raccoon Who Jumps Good

Trash Bandit (working title) is a small puzzle-platformer in the
spirit of Donkey Kong '94 for the Game Boy. So far I've spent most of
my time getting the jump mechanics to feel right. Next step is
building out actual levels and a theme.
