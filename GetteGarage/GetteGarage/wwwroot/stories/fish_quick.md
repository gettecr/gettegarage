## The Idea

Fish Quick is a fishing game where every catch is a minigame. Instead of
waiting for a bite, you earn your fish with quick-time events and
keyboard challenges. It's the minigame without the rest of the game.

Record breaking catches are stored in a database and shared with anyone else who visits
the site.

## Emojis as Art

Every fish, hook, and background is an emoji. It's a constraint I
leaned into: no art pipeline, no asset hunting, just whatever the
keyboard's emoji picker offers.
