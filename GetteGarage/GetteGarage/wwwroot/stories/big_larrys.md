## Why I Built This

This game was made as part of Tiny Mass Games! I made this game in 2 months along side several other developers that were creating games in the same time period.

Tiny Mass Games is a collective of small teams and solo-developers, mostly based in Massachusetts(-ish), who get together to develop and publish small games each quarter (visit https://tinymassgames.itch.io/). Some developers use this as a way to test ideas or get out prototypes, some use it as a bit of accountability for their projects, and others just like making small games. The framework is pretty simple. Each quarter, the group gets together to talk about games they are thinking of making with the goal of working on them and publishing in two months. Then they take a month to either continue working on the game they published (bug fixes, updates, etc.) or to come up with their next masterpiece.

Check out the Dev_log page for more details on this post: [I made and published a game in six weeks!](/blog/i-made-and-published-a-game-in-6)

## The Hard Part

Getting the pacing and balance of coding, drawing, and publishing all in a 6 week period (with a demo at indie night) was tough, but I'm glad I did it and it was super fun.
