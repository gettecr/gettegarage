# Motivation

Did you know that commercially usable birdsong for specific birds is impossible to find? Not "birdsong.mp3" but "American Robin Song.mp3" is apparently only available for purchase for educational purposes or for special circumstances. I did not know this before starting to build a game centered on real life birds. But when it came to adding some sound effects for the central characters in my game, I needed some real sounds. 

## Playing my drawing pad

Instead of buying a bunch of recording equipment and spending the next 2-5 years in the field recording the birds in my area to get authentic sounds (although don't think I wasn't tempted), I decided to use recorded bird songs and "perform" the song with a homemade instrument to make them my own. It's not a 1:1 replication of any recording and certainly doesn't do any copying. It's also kind of difficult to get it right, but I made a **birdsong instrument** with a Wacom drawing pad and converted those songs to **MIDI**. 

Then I used a custom tuned synth setting in FLStudio to try to replicate the bird sound as cleanly as possible. The interface is not pretty (yet) but I'm working on making both the instrument and UI much nicer.
